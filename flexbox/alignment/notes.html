Alignment (Flexbox) - Key Notes

  • justify-content → Aligns items on the main axis.
  • align-items → Aligns items on the cross axis.
  • gap → Creates space between flex items (better than margins for spacing).
  • flex: 1 makes items grow/shrink to fill available space.
  • Remove flex: 1 if you want items to keep their own width.
  • justify-content: center; → Centers items on the main axis.
  • justify-content: space-between; → First & last items touch edges; equal space between items.
  • align-items: center; → Centers items on the cross axis.
  • Changing flex-direction changes the meaning of the axes.
  • row (default):
    • Main axis = Horizontal
    • Cross axis = Vertical
  • column:
    • Main axis = Vertical
    • Cross axis = Horizontal
  • Always think Main Axis vs Cross Axis, not Horizontal vs Vertical.
  • Perfect Centering: 
    display: flex;
justify-content: center;
align-items: center;
